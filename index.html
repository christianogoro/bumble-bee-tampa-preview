FAQ

Quick answers

Is the in-home consultation really free?

Yes. Krista comes to your home with samples, measures your windows and gives you a clear quote. There is no cost and no obligation.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Tampa neighborhoods from Seminole Heights and Hyde Park to Temple Terrace, Gibsonton and Riverview. See the full list of neighborhoods in our service area section.

How long does it take to get my window treatments?

Everything is custom made for your windows, so timing depends on the product. We'll give you a clear timeline during your consultation.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Flexible financing is available. Ask us about current options at your consultation.

Are your products safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We offer cordless and motorized options with no dangling cords, which is the safest choice for homes with children and pets.
