About Us

Meet The Hive

Our Family

Family is at the heart of Bumble Bee Blinds. We're passionate about creating spaces that feel comfortable, welcoming, and filled with light. Outside of work, we enjoy spending time outdoors, traveling, cheering on baseball and basketball, and soaking up time with our dogs and our local community.

Bumble Bee Blinds is more than a business to us, it's our way of bringing warmth, comfort, and connection into the homes we serve. When you work with us, you become part of our hive.

The Bumble Promise

Our mission is simple: to bring warmth, light, and "bee-autiful" design to every home and business we serve.

We specialize in custom blinds, shades, and shutters, offering personalized in-home design consultations, precise measurements, and professional installation. Our goal is to make the entire process simple and seamless, from the first conversation to the final installation.

As a family-run business, relationships matter to us. We take the time to understand your needs, your style, and your space, so we can provide honest guidance, clear communication, and thoughtful solutions that enhance comfort, functionality, and style in your space.
